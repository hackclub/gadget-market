# How to Mail

Here are a couple of tips!

1. Check whether there are customs to/from the country of the person you're trading with! Gadget Market does not cover customs, so you'll have to pay it out of pocket.
2. Do not put your PCB in an envelope if it is assembled! It will be crushed. (For smaller devboards, you might be able to get away with 3D printing a protective case around it and putting it in an envelope, but please do your research beforehand.)
    - For projects less than 1/4in thick, you can get non-machinable stamps and try to put mail it in an envelope. Make sure there's nothing sharp that will stab through the paper though.
